# Sales Practice Coach Agent knowledge

## What the agent does

A Sales Practice Coach Agent roleplays approved customer personas, holds a realistic sales conversation, raises relevant objections and scores the learner against agreed selling points. It then gives specific feedback, hints and another opportunity to practise.

## Problem it removes

Partners often understand the course material but do not get enough safe practice applying it. Their first difficult conversation happens with a real customer, while managers lack the time to roleplay and coach every learner consistently.

## How it fits a business

Each brand can have its own customer personas, product knowledge, approved claims, coaching style and scoring rubric. The agent can sit inside the learning platform, use the existing login and return scores or completion data to the LMS where integration allows.

## What it needs

It needs representative customer personas, common objections, approved selling points, prohibited claims, example strong and weak conversations, scoring rules, coaching expectations, LMS reporting requirements and rules for brand and learner data separation.
