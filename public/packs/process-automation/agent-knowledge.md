# Process Automation knowledge

## What the agent does

A Process Automation agent moves routine work through the business, including lead intake, document management and email handling.

## Problem it removes

Leads arrive through forms and inboxes, sit untouched and go cold. People lose time checking, forwarding, chasing and updating systems.

## How it fits a business

The agent takes the incoming work, routes it, follows up where needed and keeps the right system current until the task is closed.
