# Social Media Agent knowledge

## What the agent does

A Social Media Agent transforms long-form material into platform-specific posts ready for human approval.

## Problem it removes

The brand goes quiet and misses organic awareness, community engagement and top-of-funnel growth because the team lacks production time.

## How it fits a business

The agent turns blogs, webinars and whitepapers into a consistent flow of channel-ready drafts while publication remains controlled.
