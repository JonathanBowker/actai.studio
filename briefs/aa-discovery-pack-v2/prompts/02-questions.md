# Discovery questions

HARD_CONSTRAINT: Each question gets exactly one push-back at most. After the probe, accept whatever comes back and move on. Never loop. Question 10's valid answer "nothing, this is new" needs no probe.

## Q1
QUESTION: What's the one business goal this needs to serve?
DESCRIPTOR: Growing revenue in one line of business. Cutting the time a job takes. Reducing errors or complaints. Winning a type of customer you don't win today. Meeting a regulation you're struggling with.
PROBE_IF: They pick an example verbatim with no detail.
PROBE: Which part of the business, and how would you measure it?

## Q2
QUESTION: What should be different in six months if it works?
DESCRIPTOR: Describe the business after, not the project. Fewer people chasing paperwork. Content going out weekly instead of monthly. Tenants getting answers the same day.
PROBE_IF: The answer is vague.
PROBE: What would you physically notice walking in on a Monday?

## Q3
QUESTION: How will you know? What number moves?
DESCRIPTOR: Days to complete. Enquiries per week. Cost per job. Views, sign-ups, repeat orders.
PROBE_IF: No metric is given.
PROBE: If you don't track that yet, what would you want to start counting?

## Q4
QUESTION: Who owns this day to day, and who signs it off?
DESCRIPTOR: The person who'd use it every day, and the person who approves the money and the risk.
PROBE_IF: One name only is given.
PROBE: And who signs off the budget or the risk, if that's someone else?

## Q5
QUESTION: Where does the information for this live now, and who holds it?
DESCRIPTOR: Spreadsheets, email threads, a CRM, a shared drive, paper files, someone's head.
PROBE_IF: A system name only is given.
PROBE: Is that complete, or is some of it still in someone's head or in email?

## Q6
QUESTION: What rules does this work have to respect?
DESCRIPTOR: Brand guidelines, contracts and licences, sector regulation, data protection, claims you can't make.
PROBE_IF: The answer is "nothing really" or "the usual".
PROBE: What's the one rule that, if broken, would actually cause a problem?

## Q7
QUESTION: What systems or channels does it touch, and which can't change?
DESCRIPTOR: Accounts package, property system, website, social channels, email.
PROBE_IF: Systems are named but no constraints are given.
PROBE: Which of those is fixed, and which would you happily replace?

## Q8
QUESTION: Who on your team would work alongside it, and what do they know?
DESCRIPTOR: What they're good at, what they're tired of doing, what knowledge isn't written down anywhere.
PROBE_IF: A role only is given.
PROBE: What do they know that isn't written down anywhere?

## Q9
QUESTION: What's the first small version you'd accept?
DESCRIPTOR: One report. One video. One step handled without a human. Small enough to see in weeks.
PROBE_IF: The answer describes the whole finished thing.
PROBE: What's the smallest piece of that you'd be glad to see working first?

## Q10
QUESTION: What's got in the way before?
DESCRIPTOR: Previous attempts, software that never got used, agencies that didn't land, people who pushed back.
PROBE_IF: none. "Nothing, this is new" is a valid answer.
PROBE: none
