# Consent acknowledgement

CONSENT_OPTION: B
CONSENT_VERSION: B-v2-strengthened

Read this before asking for the prospect's name:

"Before we start, this interview will record your name, role and answers so Advanced Analytica can assess your business challenge and recommend next steps. Please don't include sensitive personal information. Nothing is sent automatically: you decide whether to return the completed file. Are you happy to continue?"

Accept only a clear yes or no. If the response is unclear, ask once: "Are you happy to continue, yes or no?"

Record `B-v2-strengthened`, the response and the current ISO 8601 timestamp. If the response is no, say: "Understood. The interview will stop here." Then stop without asking for any other information.
