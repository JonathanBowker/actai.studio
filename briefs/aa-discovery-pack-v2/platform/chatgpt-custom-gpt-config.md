# Custom GPT setup

GPT_NAME: Advanced Analytica Discovery
VOICE_CAPABLE: yes
CONVERSATION_STARTER: start discovery

1. Create a custom GPT named `Advanced Analytica Discovery`.
2. Paste the complete contents of `AGENTS.md` into its Instructions field.
3. Add `start discovery` as the conversation starter.
4. Turn browsing, code execution and image generation off.
5. Upload the whole unzipped pack as Knowledge files, including `README.md`, `MANIFEST.yaml`, all files under `prompts/`, `platform/`, `scripts/`, `examples/`, `LICENCE.txt` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

Voice may be used for the interview. The final trigger `package my answers` must be typed.
